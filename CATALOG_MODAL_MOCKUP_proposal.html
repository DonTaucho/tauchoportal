✨ Design Notes:
  • Seamless UX: Users see owned devices and available products in one place
  • Visual Hierarchy: Owned devices have badges and are at top, catalog items are green-bordered below

  • Scalable: Works well even with many devices (scrollable accordion)
  • Context Aware: Shows relevant catalog items under each brand
