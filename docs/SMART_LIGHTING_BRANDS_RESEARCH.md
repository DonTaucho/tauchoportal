## About Your Current Brands

### ✅ Keep These (Output-Focused)

- **Yeelight** ✓ Major budget option in Asia/worldwide
- **WLED** ✓ Open-source community standard

### ⚠️ Reconsider These

#### **Tuya** - NOT recommended as primary brand
**Why:** 
- Tuya is primarily an **IoT cloud platform/API service** for other makers
- Tuya does make some output devices (lights, plugs), but they're not a "brand" in the traditional sense
- Most Tuya devices are rebranded/white-labeled by other companies
- If you want to support "Tuya the platform," it should be for API integration, not as a catalog brand
- **Recommendation:** If you want support Tuya devices, create this as **"Third-Party Tuya Devices"** or **"Smart Life App"** instead of treating Tuya as a primary brand

#### **Wyze** - MIXED focus (not primarily output)
**Why:**
- Wyze is primarily known for **security cameras and sensors** (input devices)
- Their smart lighting line exists but is secondary/minimal
- They have basic bulbs and strips, but this is NOT their main market
- **Recommendation:** If you want Wyze support, specify it's for their lights specifically, but understand it's not their core business

#### **Amazon Alexa** - NOT a product brand
**Why:**
- Alexa is a **platform/voice assistant service**, not a smart lighting brand
- Amazon does make "Amazon Basics" smart bulbs (which are budget options)
- **Recommendation:** Either rename to **"Amazon Basics"** (their actual product line) or remove if you mean Alexa as just a control platform
